I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV) and a
predoctoral researcher at the eSmart Research Center.

My research interests lie in applied economics, particularly at the intersection of econometrics
and machine learning. As part of the **EVALIAX** project, I work on public policy evaluation,
combining causal inference with explainable AI (XAI) to make policy assessments both rigorous
and interpretable. Most of my current work uses Spanish administrative registries — the national
subsidy database (BDNS) linked to firm accounts — to estimate what public support programmes
actually do to the firms that receive them.

Before starting the PhD I spent four years as a data scientist in industry and at the Spanish
independent fiscal authority (AIReF), which is where the interest in evaluating public policy
with credible methods started.
