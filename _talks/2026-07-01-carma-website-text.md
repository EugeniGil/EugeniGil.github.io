Paper presented at **CARMA 2026** (Universitat Politècnica de València, 1–3 July 2026). Joint
work with Pablo de Pedraza and Ana Garcia-Bernabeu.

Standard evaluation data describe how large a firm is and which sector it belongs to, but say
almost nothing about what it actually does. The talk asked whether the text of a firm's own
website can stand in for those unobserved characteristics when building counterfactuals, and
showed that semantic embeddings of website content carry **27.66%** of the predictive weight in a
Variable Importance Matching procedure applied to **Kit Digital** — non-redundant information
that balance sheets do not contain, obtained without loss of covariate balance.
